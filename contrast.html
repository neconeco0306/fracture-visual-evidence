THE IMPORTANT CAVEAT

Numbers are not
an audit.

WCAG 2.2 uses 4.5:1 for ordinary text and 3:1 for large text at AA; 7:1 and 4.5:1 at AAA. But a screenshot can include antialiasing, shadows, transparency, and compression. Two picked pixels cannot certify an entire interface.
